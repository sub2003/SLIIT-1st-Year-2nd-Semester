Question 01

A Hospital Management System manages different types of medical staff and patient treatment records. Each person has a name and contact number, but there are specialized people such as Doctors and Nurses who have unique responsibilities.

Each TreatmentRecord contains a MedicalReport that stores the diagnosis details, meaning the MedicalReport is part of the TreatmentRecord and cannot exist without it.

Additionally, the hospital has multiple Patients, and each Patient is associated with a TreatmentRecord. However, a Patient can exist independently even if there is no treatment record.

Different types of medical staff have unique implementations of the performDuty() method, which need to be overridden in the respective classes.

a) Implement a class called MedicalStaff with staffID(String), name(String), and contactNumber(String) attributes.
i. Create a method called performDuty() that prints a generic message.

b) Create two classes called Doctor and Nurse as subclasses of MedicalStaff.
i. Override the performDuty() method to display unique messages for each type.

c) Implement a MedicalReport class with diagnosis(String) and reportDate(String) as attributes.
i. The MedicalReport should be part of a TreatmentRecord using Composition and initialized within the TreatmentRecord constructor.

d) Create a class called Patient with patientID(String), patientName(String), and age(int) attributes.
i. A Patient should be associated with a TreatmentRecord using Aggregation.

e) Modify the TreatmentRecord class to include a Patient and a MedicalStaff member.
i. The staff member may be a Doctor or Nurse.

f) Create a HospitalApp class with a main method to:
i. Create objects for different medical staff members.
ii. Create patients and assign them to treatment records.
iii. Display full treatment details, including the patient, staff member, and medical report.
